How the app runs

Burn-up for Jira is built on Atlassian Forge. Its code and data are hosted by Atlassian, inside Atlassian’s cloud. Catoira Consulting Inc runs no servers for the app, and the app makes no calls to anything outside Atlassian: no external services, analytics or trackers. It is a Forge-only app, with no Connect modules.

What the app reads

To draw a chart for an Epic or a release, the app reads from your Jira site, with the permissions of the person viewing it:

  • the Epic or release (key, and summary or name) and its issues: keys, created dates, status and status history, and the story points field when story points are used;
  • for issues that used to belong to the Epic or release, the history of their parent or fix version, so removed issues show up to the week they left;
  • site settings needed to draw the chart: statuses, the site time zone, number fields (for the story points picker), issue types, and whether the viewer is an admin.

The app does not store issue summaries, descriptions, comments, attachments or other issue fields, nor people’s names, email addresses or avatars, nor any password or token. The one exception is the key and summary (or name) of the Epic or release itself, which is the chart’s title.

Who can see what

  • The app is read-only. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in Jira.
  • Charts are read as the signed-in person, so Jira’s own permissions decide what each person sees. The chart cache is per viewer and is never shown to anyone else.
  • Large charts are built in the background as the app, but only from issue ids the requesting viewer’s own search found. Findings from the scan for removed issues are re-checked as the viewer before they are used, and the scan’s progress reaches the panel as a percentage only.
  • Changing settings is limited to Jira admins (site) and project admins (project overrides).
  • Catoira Consulting Inc has no access to your Jira site or to the app’s stored data.

There are no write scopes for Jira data.

Data residency

The app’s data is kept in Atlassian’s Forge hosted storage, which supports Atlassian data residency. It stays in the same location as your Jira site, and if the site’s data is moved to another location, the app’s data moves automatically with it.

Logs

Atlassian keeps operational logs for Forge apps, which we can read. The app’s production logs contain only ids, counts, durations and error types (for example an HTTP status), never issue content.

Retention and deletion

  • Settings, chart inputs and past-member ids are kept until the app is uninstalled. Charts are cached for 15 minutes, and background job records are deleted after 12 hours.
  • If a subscription or trial ends, nothing is deleted; the app asks for an active license until you subscribe again.
  • Uninstalling: Atlassian soft-deletes the app’s stored data and keeps it for a limited period under its data retention policy, then deletes it. A reinstall is treated as a new installation, but if it is requested within 21 days of uninstalling, the new installation can be relinked to the previous data (see Atlassian’s hosted storage data lifecycle).

Our practices

  • The calculation engine is separate from Jira and Forge code and is covered by automated tests, including reopened issues, Won’t Do and Duplicate resolutions, and unestimated issues.
  • Dependencies are installed from a lockfile.
  • The app follows the principle of least privilege: it requests only the permissions listed above.

Report a security issue

Email support@catoira.app with “Security report” in the subject. Include the steps to reproduce, the impact you see, and how to reach you. We acknowledge reports within three business days and keep you updated until the issue is fixed. Please give us a reasonable time to fix an issue before disclosing it publicly, and don’t access or change data that isn’t yours while testing.
