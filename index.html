From Epic to forecast in four steps

No spreadsheet and no copies of your issues. The chart is built from your Jira history, so it is always true to what happened.

  1. Open an Epic or release

    Use the Burn-up panel on any Epic, or pick a release from the Burn-up page in your project.

  2. Choose a metric

    Count issues (throughput) or story points. Pick one per chart.

  3. Enter your weekly capacity

    Add a start date and a deadline if you have them. Hints show what your team actually finished lately.

  4. Read the forecast

    See the predicted end date, and whether you are on track or late. Copy the chart as an image to share it.

Built to answer one question: when will this finish?

Built from your history
The app replays each issue’s changelog, so reopened issues, work finished before the start date and issues removed from the Epic all show in the right week.
Scrum and Kanban
Capacity is always per week, so the same chart works for sprints, flow and anything in between.
Predicted end date
The forecast climbs at your planned capacity until it meets today’s scope, and the end date is marked on the chart.
Ideal line to the deadline
Set a deadline and see the straight line you would need to follow, plus the capacity that would hit it.
Planned, observed and needed
Compare the capacity you planned, the last six weeks’ average and what the deadline demands, side by side.
Copy as an image
One click copies the chart as a PNG for Slack, email or a slide. Download PNG is there too.
